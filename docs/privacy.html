Your data and Training Coach

Last updated October 16, 2026

About this app

Training Coach is a student project built by Clive Curry for an AI seminar class at Wake Forest. It plans each day's training from your WHOOP data, the weather, and your Google Calendar. It gives training suggestions, not medical advice.

What it collects

  • Your Google name and email, so it knows who you are when you sign in.
  • Your WHOOP recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, strain, and workouts, but only after you choose to connect WHOOP.
  • Your Google Calendar event titles and times, but only if you connect your calendar. The coach uses them to find free time. It adds a workout only after you approve it, and it never changes or deletes your other events.
  • The city you enter, to look up the weather.
  • Your messages to the coach, and short notes the coach saves from them (like an injury you mention). You can see, edit, or delete the notes on your Coaching profile page.

How it's used

  • Only to show you your own data and to plan your training.
  • When you ask for a plan or message the coach, your recent numbers, the calendar events it needs, and the coach's notes are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which writes the answer.
  • On the demo page, messages you type to the coach are sent to Anthropic's Claude API together with the made-up sample data, so it can answer. This site doesn't save them.
  • Your data is never sold, never used for ads, and never shared with anyone except as described here.
  • Training Coach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How it's protected

  • It's stored in a database that only the website's server can open.
  • Your WHOOP and Google login tokens are encrypted before they're saved.
  • The site's font (Inter) loads from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page opens.

Deleting your data

In the app, sign in and use Delete my data on the Privacy page. It permanently deletes everything the app has saved about you. You can also remove the app's access from inside the WHOOP app, and from your Google Account's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
